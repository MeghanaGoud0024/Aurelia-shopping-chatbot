# Returns, Exchanges and Refunds

## Return window

Items may be returned within **30 calendar days of delivery**. The window is measured from the delivered date recorded against the order, not the order date. Loyalty members at Gold and Platinum tiers receive an extended window of 60 days.

## Condition requirements

To be accepted, a returned item must be:

- Unworn and unwashed, with no odour, marks or pet hair
- In its original packaging where packaging forms part of the product, such as footwear boxes and watch cases
- With all original tags and hygiene seals still attached

Footwear must be tried on indoors, on a clean surface. A sole showing outdoor wear will be rejected at inspection.

## Non-returnable items

The following cannot be returned unless faulty:

- Underwear, swimwear and socks where the hygiene seal has been broken
- Pierced jewellery
- Gift cards
- Items marked **Final Sale** on the product page
- Personalised or custom-embroidered items

## How to start a return

Returns are initiated from the order in the customer account, or through this assistant, on any order in `delivered` status that is inside its return window. A prepaid return label is issued by email. The order moves to `return_requested` and then to `returned` once the warehouse completes inspection.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

| Payment method | Refund lands within |
| --- | --- |
| Card | 5 to 10 business days after inspection |
| UPI | 3 to 5 business days |
| Net banking | 5 to 7 business days |
| Wallet | Immediately on inspection |
| Cash on delivery | 5 to 7 business days to a nominated bank account |

Original shipping fees are refunded only when the return is the result of an Aurelia error, such as a wrong or faulty item. Return postage is free on the first return per order; subsequent returns against the same order carry a 4.99 USD label fee.

## Exchanges

Aurelia does not process direct exchanges. The fastest route to a different size or colour is to place a new order and return the original, so that the replacement is not held up by inspection.

## Faulty items

An item that fails within its warranty period is treated as faulty, not as a change of mind. Faulty items are refunded or replaced in full, including all shipping costs, regardless of the 30-day window.

# Warranty and Product Care

## Warranty periods

| Category | Warranty | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apparel (topwear, bottomwear, outerwear) | 6 months | Seam failure, fabric defects, zip and fastening failure |
| Footwear | 6 months | Sole separation, upper failure, stitching defects |
| Watches | 24 months | Movement and mechanical failure |
| Sunglasses | 12 months | Hinge and frame defects, lens delamination |
| Bags and backpacks | 12 months | Strap, zip and stitching failure |

Warranty runs from the delivered date recorded against the order.

## What warranty does not cover

Normal wear, including sole tread wear, pilling, fading from washing and UV exposure, and scuffing. Damage from misuse, accident or alteration. Items where care instructions were not followed, such as machine washing a dry-clean-only garment.

## Making a warranty claim

Warranty claims are handled by Aurelia Customer Care and require the order number and photographs of the defect. Claims are assessed within 3 business days. An upheld claim is resolved by repair, replacement or full refund, at Aurelia's discretion, at no cost to the customer.

## Product care

Following the care label materially extends garment life. Wash performance fabrics cold and avoid fabric softener, which coats technical fibres and destroys moisture-wicking behaviour. Air-dry outerwear with a water-repellent finish rather than tumble drying at high heat. Store leather footwear with a shoe tree and away from direct heat.

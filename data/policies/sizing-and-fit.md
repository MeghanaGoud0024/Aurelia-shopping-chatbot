# Sizing and Fit

## Apparel size chart

Measurements are body measurements in centimetres, not garment measurements.

| Size | Chest | Waist | Hip |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| XS | 86 to 91 | 71 to 76 | 86 to 91 |
| S | 91 to 97 | 76 to 81 | 91 to 97 |
| M | 97 to 102 | 81 to 87 | 97 to 102 |
| L | 102 to 109 | 87 to 94 | 102 to 109 |
| XL | 109 to 117 | 94 to 102 | 109 to 117 |
| XXL | 117 to 125 | 102 to 110 | 117 to 125 |

## Fit descriptions

- **Slim Fit** sits close to the body. Size up if you are between sizes or prefer room through the chest.
- **Regular Fit** is the standard cut and is true to the chart above.
- **Athletic Fit** is cut wider at the shoulder and chest and tapered at the waist.
- **Relaxed Fit** adds roughly 4 cm of ease over Regular.
- **Oversized** is a deliberate style choice, typically two sizes of ease. Take your normal size.

## Footwear

Aurelia lists footwear in US sizing. Running shoes from Nike and Adidas run approximately half a size small; sizing up by half is the common recommendation. New Balance and Under Armour run true to size. Sandals run large; sizing down by half is recommended.

## Brand variation

Fast-fashion labels vary more than performance labels. Zara and H&M run approximately one size small relative to the chart. Uniqlo runs true to size. Tommy Hilfiger and Calvin Klein run generous through the body.

## If the size is wrong

Sizing is the single most common reason for a return, and it is treated as a change of mind rather than a fault. The standard 30-day return window applies. Aurelia does not process direct exchanges; place a new order in the correct size and return the original.

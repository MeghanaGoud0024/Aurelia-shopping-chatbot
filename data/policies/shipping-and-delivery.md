# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery speeds and cost

Aurelia offers three delivery speeds. Cut-off for same-day dispatch is 14:00 local time in the destination market, Monday to Friday excluding public holidays.

| Service | Delivery window (business days) | Cost | Free above |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 4 to 7 | 7.99 USD | 75.00 USD |
| Express | 2 to 3 | 14.99 USD | 150.00 USD |
| Priority | 1 (metro postcodes only) | 24.99 USD | Not available |

Delivery windows are counted from the moment the order reaches `packed`, not from the moment it is placed. Orders placed after the cut-off, on a weekend, or on a public holiday begin counting from the next business day.

## Estimated delivery dates

Every order that has been confirmed carries an estimated delivery date. The estimate is generated by the fulfilment system from the destination postcode, the selected service and current carrier performance. It is an estimate, not a guarantee. Orders in `pending_payment` have no estimate, because the dispatch clock has not started.

Priority is the only service that carries a delivery guarantee. If a Priority order is not delivered by end of the promised day, the shipping fee is automatically refunded within two business days.

## Tracking

A tracking number is issued when the carrier scans the parcel at the origin hub, which is the transition into `shipped`. Before that point the order is genuinely still in the warehouse and no tracking number exists. Tracking events refresh approximately every four hours.

## International shipping

Aurelia ships to 34 markets. International orders may attract import duties and taxes levied by the destination country. These are payable by the recipient and are not collected by Aurelia at checkout. Customs clearance can add 2 to 5 business days and is outside carrier control.

## Failed delivery

Couriers make two delivery attempts. After a second failure the parcel is held at a local depot for 7 calendar days, then returned to Aurelia. Returned-to-sender parcels are refunded in full, minus the original shipping fee, once received back at the warehouse.

## Address changes

A shipping address can be amended while the order is in `confirmed`. Once the order reaches `packed` the label is printed and the address is locked. After that point the only route is to refuse delivery and reorder.
